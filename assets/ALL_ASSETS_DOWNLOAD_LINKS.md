### **2. Voice Guidance - Text-to-Speech (Recommended)**

Instead of recording or downloading, use Flutter TTS:

**Google Cloud Text-to-Speech**
🔗 https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech
- English (Indian accent)
- Hindi (multiple voices)
- 1 million characters free per month

**Implementation in Flutter:**
```dart
import 'package:flutter_tts/flutter_tts.dart';

FlutterTts tts = FlutterTts();
await tts.setLanguage("en-IN"); // or "hi-IN" for Hindi
await tts.speak("Welcome to Khetopedia");
```

**Alternative:** Record your own voice using Audacity (free software)
🔗 https://www.audacityteam.org/
